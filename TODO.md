## Reminders

Raise these at the **start of every session**, before whatever else was asked. Keep it to a line or two, then get on with the actual request.

A reminder can carry a constraint in parentheses before the text — `(from YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM)` is the common one, and anything else in there is a plain-English condition. Don't raise it until the constraint is met.
